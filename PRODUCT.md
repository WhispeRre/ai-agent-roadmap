# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

This project serves Chinese and bilingual learners who want a structured path into AI Agent engineering. They may be developers, students, product builders, or researchers who need a readable roadmap rather than a scattered collection of links.

## Product Purpose

AI Agent Roadmap is a personal learning handbook and practice roadmap. It organizes Agent concepts, engineering patterns, papers, and hands-on projects into a maintained mdBook site. Success means readers can understand the field map, choose a learning path, and return to the project as a practical reference while building.

## Brand Personality

Clear, deliberate, and study-friendly. The project should feel like a maintained technical notebook: calm enough for long reading, structured enough for repeated reference, and personal enough to show curation rather than a generic documentation dump.

## Anti-references

Avoid pretending the project is fully original when it builds on open-source material. Avoid dark SaaS dashboards, loud gradient-heavy AI branding, vague hype copy, and decorative visuals that make long-form reading harder.

## Design Principles

- Lead with learning value: make the roadmap, reading paths, and project scope visible before badges or promotion.
- Be transparent about provenance: keep license obligations and acknowledgements clear while emphasizing personal curation and improvements.
- Optimize for long sessions: typography, spacing, navigation, and contrast should support focused reading.
- Make structure feel intentional: chapter lists, tables, callouts, and code blocks should help readers decide what to read next.
- Keep the visual system memorable but quiet: use color for orientation and emphasis, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for normal text. Support reduced motion preferences, keyboard navigation, and comfortable reading on mobile and desktop. Chinese content is primary, with English content retained for bilingual readers.
